# Bloom Proof — Vision

## The one-liner

Bloom Proof is a public record of AI doing real work for real people — every entry links a working project, or it doesn't get in.

## What it is

The internet is full of AI hype, and it is hard to tell what is real. Bloom Proof keeps one simple promise: every story here links to a real project you can click, try, and learn from — checked by a human before it appears.

Each entry has five parts: the **Problem** (real), the **Solution** (working AI), the **Artifact** (URL to the working thing — repo, live demo, dataset, or model weights), the **Human** (who it helped), and the **License** (open).

The entry tells the story. The artifact is the evidence. A reader clicks it and verifies it themselves. Without the artifact, it's just a story anyone could invent. With it, it's proof.

## How it works

1. Someone finds AI doing real work and writes it up as a markdown file in `entries/`, following the template.
2. The one rule is the checklist: real problem, working artifact, open license, human helped. No working artifact, no entry.
3. A maintainer verifies the artifact link is alive and the license is open, then merges. Merge = published.
4. The site rebuilds from `entries/` automatically. The record grows.

Anyone can fork the repo and host their own copy free. No servers, no database, no permission needed. That is the design — no one can take it down or buy it.

## Who it is for

- **Visitors:** find tools that actually work, win arguments with skeptics, get ideas for your own builds.
- **Contributors:** submit entries via pull request, or improve the site itself — same repo.
- **Builders featured in entries:** recognition and distribution for work that matters.
- **Journalists and researchers:** a citable public record with checkable artifacts.
- **Funders:** a map of what is actually working, so money can follow results.

## Money and capture

There is nothing to buy. The code is MIT, the content is CC BY-SA 4.0. No equity, no tokens, no board seats, no control for sale.

Funding is welcome as grants, no-strings sponsorships, or direct support to the projects *in* the record rather than the record itself. The record stays un-capturable — that is a founding principle, not a negotiating position.

## Terminology

This project says **AI** because it is the word the world uses and searches for. Related terms readers may meet elsewhere:

- **SI — Super Intelligence:** since September 2026, the US executive branch uses this in place of AI (Executive Order 14434). Same systems, new name.
- **SI — Synthetic Intelligence:** used by some builders who feel "artificial" sounds fake. Same technology, different framing.
- **AGI — Artificial General Intelligence:** human-level ability across domains. A goal, not a product.
- **ASI — Artificial Superintelligence:** systems smarter than humans at everything. Hypothetical — not what exists today.

The work is the same whatever you call it.

## Where it is going

1. **The record** — collect entries. This is now.
2. **The standard** — "Bloom Proof verified" becomes a badge projects want, cited by press and researchers.
3. **The map** — funders and builders coordinate from it; resources flow to what works.
4. **The permanent layer** — mirrored everywhere, decentralized storage, un-capturable.

In ten years, when someone asks "is AI good for the world?", the answer isn't an essay. It's bloomproof.org.
