# 35 million cells, explorable by any biologist

## Problem

Single-cell datasets exploded to millions of cells across thousands of genes — but exploring them required serious coding, locking most biologists out of data that could reveal how diseases work at the cellular level.

## Solution

CZI's CELLxGENE Discover is a no-code, in-browser platform for exploring more than 35 million cells across 1,000+ standardized datasets: interactive gene-expression visualization, comparison between cell groups, and an encyclopedia of 700+ cell types — all point-and-click, no install.

## Artifact

- Live site: https://cellxgene.cziscience.com — browse 1,000+ datasets, run interactive gene-expression analyses, explore the cell encyclopedia, all in the browser
- Code: https://github.com/chanzuckerberg/cellxgene — the open-source platform
- Open license: MIT (declared LICENSE file, verified 2026-10-14)

## Human

The Tabula Sapiens Consortium — more than 150 researchers across institutions who built a 24-organ human cell atlas on the platform — plus disease researchers using it to find which tissues COVID-19 attacks and which cell types drive lupus. Phil Smoot, CZI's head of science and technology: the tool is helping scientists "answer fundamental questions about human biology in seconds, not years."
