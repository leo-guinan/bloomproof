# A plant doctor in every farmer's pocket

## Problem

Cassava feeds over half a billion people a day in Africa. It gets sick slowly — a farmer can't tell which disease is on the leaf until it's already spread to the neighbor's farm. Trained experts are few and far away.

## Solution

A free phone app called Nuru — "light" in Swahili. Point the camera at a leaf. In seconds the AI names the disease, over 90% accuracy, better than trained human extension agents in field tests. It runs fully offline on cheap Android phones. No signal, no cloud, no cost. Then it tells the farmer what to do about it.

## Artifact

- App: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=plantvillage.nuru — Nuru on Google Play: free, runs fully offline, live since 2018
- Code: https://plantvillage.psu.edu — the 200,000 annotated cassava images from Penn State's PlantVillage that trained it; openly published, CC BY-SA
- Open license: CC BY-SA (PlantVillage training dataset)
- Open stack: built on open-source TensorFlow

## Human

Smallholder farmers in Kenya (Busia and Bungoma), Tanzania, Uganda, and Nigeria. Penn State's team also reaches ~500,000 Kenyan farmers by SMS with advice from the same system. PlantVillage founder David Hughes: the most satisfying part "has been to make a difference in the lives of the poorest people on the planet."
