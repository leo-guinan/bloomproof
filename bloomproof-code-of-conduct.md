# Code of Conduct

## Be a bloomer

- Be kind. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- No harassment, hate speech, or personal attacks — ever.
- No doom. We're here to prove what works.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove entries, close PRs, or ban contributors who violate this code. Decisions are public and explained.

## The spirit

Bloom over doom. Build the receipts.
